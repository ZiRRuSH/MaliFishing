# 🎣 MaliFishing

A lean, mean fishing machine for WoW Forever. Cast and reel with one key, see what’s biting, and find out what your time on the water is worth. I built MaliFishing to stay lightweight: the features you’ll actually use, none of the fluff.

## 💰 Fishing & loot

- One-key fishing. Use the same keybind to cast and reel in, or enable double right-click to fish with the mouse.
- Automatic rod and lure handling. Optionally auto equip your best usable fishing rod when you press your fishing bind and apply your selected lure. Restore your previous weapons after 30 seconds of fishing inactivity or when you enter combat. Makes fishing fluid and effortless when you roll up on a spot or pool!
- Fresh-catch container opening. Your next fishing input can open one eligible clam, crate, trunk, or bloated fish. MaliFishing uses the game’s `hasLoot` flag instead of a hardcoded item list and leaves locked containers and older bag stock alone.
- Hear the bobber over the background. An optional splash boost raises sound effects and lowers ambience while your line is out, then restores what it changed without touching music or master volume.
- Vendor and optional Auctionator data reference to see what your time on the water is worth.

## 📊 Catch stats & value

- Catch odds for everything you pull up. See counts and percentages for every bobber catch, not just fish. Browse session or all-time history by area or zone. Loot from inside containers doesn’t count toward the odds.
- Live fishing stats. See total catches, catches per hour, elapsed time, and vendor value per hour. With Auctionator, see estimated auction value per hour, marked as partial when some catches don’t have prices.
- Track the catch you came for. Right-click a catch in the breakdown to pin its icon and hourly rate to the HUD. Right-click the pinned row to clear it.

## 🎨 HUD & controls

- Two HUDs, one shortcut. Use the regular HUD for the full breakdown or the minimal HUD for just the stats you want.
- A minimal HUD you can actually make minimal. Toggle vendor value, Auctionator estimate, catches per hour, and target catch independently.
- Customizable to get both HUDs looking how you want and showing the info you want to see.
